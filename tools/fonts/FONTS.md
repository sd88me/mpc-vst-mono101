# Fonts

- `Univers_53_Extended_Regular.otf`: the panel lettering, emboldened by a stroke and fitted to Lemus's measured legend
  widths. The face of our other Roland ports (Model-303, Acid, JV-880). Univers is a Linotype typeface.
- `Earth_Normal.ttf`: the Roland ports' wordmark face (not drawn on the panel tabs yet).
- `LiberationSans-Bold.ttf` (OFL): Helvetica-metric fallback.
- `TitilliumWeb-*.ttf` (OFL): the first skin's face, unused now.

Confirm the Univers and Earth Normal licences allow redistributing the files (or baking them into the skin images)
before a public release.
